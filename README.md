# 🌱 WarmTodo

### Things I Have Done

WarmTodo is a warm and minimal productivity app designed to help users manage tasks, build routines, track progress, and reflect on personal growth.

Unlike traditional to-do apps that only focus on unfinished tasks, WarmTodo believes every completed task is a meaningful milestone. It helps users record not only **what they need to do**, but also **what they have achieved and how they feel along the way**.

---

## ✨ Features

### ✅ Task Management
- Create and organize daily tasks
- Support one-time tasks and long-term goals
- Track progress and completion status
- Keep completed tasks as personal achievements

### 📅 Calendar View
- Visualize tasks through a calendar interface
- Review past activities and future plans
- Build awareness of your time and progress

### 🔄 Routine Management
- Create recurring habits and routines
- Track consistency over time
- Turn small actions into lasting changes

### 🌿 Personal Reflection
- Record feelings and thoughts after completing tasks
- Review your journey through reflections
- Transform productivity into personal growth

### 📊 Progress Overview
- View completion statistics
- Understand your working patterns
- Celebrate small achievements

---

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

WarmTodo is built around a simple idea:

> Productivity is not only about finishing more tasks, but also about understanding yourself through the process.

The design follows a warm, minimal, and calming aesthetic, creating a more human-centered experience compared with traditional productivity tools.
